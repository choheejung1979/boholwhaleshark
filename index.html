Frequently Asked Questions

Will I receive a QR ticket?

Yes. The production version is designed to issue a unique QR e-ticket after successful payment. This prototype shows the intended flow.

Can I pay with GCash or Maya?

The checkout layout supports GCash, Maya, cards, and QR Ph. Final payment methods depend on the methods enabled on the connected PayMongo account.

Is this page already connected to live payments?

No. This file is the working front-end prototype. Live PayMongo checkout, inventory limits, confirmation email, and QR validation require backend integration.
