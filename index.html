About the experience

See it. Respect it. Protect it.

Guest rules

Respect the ocean.

Final rules will be updated to match the applicable Bohol local regulations and operating guidelines.

DO

  • Follow guide and boat crew instructions.
  • Enter the water calmly when instructed.
  • Keep a respectful distance from wildlife.
  • Use reef-safe sun protection where possible.
  • Respect all local marine regulations.

DON'T

  • Do not touch, chase or block a whale shark.
  • Do not feed marine wildlife.
  • Do not use flash or disruptive underwater lights.
  • Do not throw anything into the sea.
  • Do not enter the water without permission.
