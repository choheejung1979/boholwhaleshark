Marine wildlife

About Whale Sharks

What is a whale shark?

A whale shark is a very large filter-feeding shark species. It is a fish, not a whale, and feeds mainly on small marine organisms rather than large prey.

Are whale shark sightings guaranteed?

No wildlife encounter should be treated as guaranteed. Final sighting, cancellation and rebooking policies will follow the confirmed Bohol operator and local-government rules before opening.

Can guests touch or chase whale sharks?

No. Guests should never touch, chase, ride, feed, block or deliberately disturb whale sharks. Final encounter-distance and interaction rules will be updated to match the applicable Bohol regulations.

Responsible encounter

Encounter & Safety

Is swimming with whale sharks safe?

Whale sharks are generally non-aggressive filter feeders, but this remains an open-water wildlife activity. Guests must follow crew instructions, safety briefings and all local encounter rules.

What actions are prohibited?

Touching, chasing, feeding, riding, blocking or intentionally disturbing wildlife should not be permitted. Flash photography and other restrictions will follow the final local operating guidelines.

Are there age or health restrictions?

Final age, health and participation restrictions are not yet confirmed. They will be published before bookings become live.
