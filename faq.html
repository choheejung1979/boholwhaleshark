Frequently Asked Questions

Booking, safety, preparation, weather and operating information for your Bohol whale shark visit.

When does the season open?

The current opening notice is November 1, 2026. Final operating updates can be added here before launch.

Where is the meeting point?

The exact check-in location will be confirmed before opening.

What should I bring?

Bring swimwear, a towel, a change of clothes, and reef-safe sunscreen.

How do I receive my ticket?

After the payment system is connected, guests will receive a booking confirmation and QR e-ticket.

What happens in bad weather?

Operations may be delayed, rescheduled, or cancelled when sea conditions are unsafe.
